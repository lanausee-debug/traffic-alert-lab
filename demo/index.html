What the metrics mean

Proxy event and lead time
Three consecutive valid 5-minute speed readings at or below 30 mph define the low-speed state. Lead follows L = 5(s − a + 1), where s is the event's first positive position and a is the prediction origin. Real congestion onset has not been independently verified.
Detection and false alerts
Detection includes matched late alerts. False alerts count new unmatched notifications per naturally evaluable station-day, not pointwise false-positive rate. Repeated and unscorable alerts are separate categories.
Evaluation scope
All settings were selected on calibration data. This explorer reads aggregate frozen fine-grid results. Artificial outages hide historical inputs only. Three scenarios replay the same events and do not create independent extra events.
